Our analysis framework

Every match preview should explain the reasoning behind the pick.

  • History: head-to-head, player, manager and team records.
  • Records: milestones that could be reached or broken.
  • Patterns: winning/losing runs, scoring trends and clean sheets.
  • Current form: recent performances, injuries and availability.
  • Final call: prediction, key risks and confidence level.

How picks are produced

A hybrid approach: human judgement plus data-assisted checks.

  • Manual: benPitch reviews the match and writes the explanation.
  • Data-assisted: fixture, form and statistics feeds can support the review once connected.
  • Quality check: publish the source and update the result after the match.

Automated data is not connected in this starter version. No live stats or AI-generated picks are being claimed.
